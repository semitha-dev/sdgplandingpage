01 / Our services

One workflow. From intake to release.

Purpose-built tools for sea-cargo consolidation between Japan and Sri Lanka.

Register & consolidate

Capture sender, consignee and cargo details under each HBL. Group consignments into containers and record every package loaded.

Prepare & communicate

Generate shipping documents and invoices, prepare Sri Lanka Customs output, and keep recipients informed with messaging and public tracking.

Collect, release & dispatch

Record counter payments, issue receipts and reconcile daily cash. Manage arrival, unloading, bonded warehouse release and last-mile driver assignment.
